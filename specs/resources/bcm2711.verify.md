# Verification of `bcm2711` (GPL overlay)

Delta pass: the spec moved from 89557f7 to d4721e5, and `git diff 89557f7 d4721e5` touches only
Quick-facts/3 and Quick-facts/5 (both FAIL in the earlier record). Those two were verified fresh;
every other line is carried unchanged from the 89557f7 record. Mechanical checks at d4721e5:
`anchor_check.py --root <this root> --require-license` with `linux` and `linux-pcie` given the
checkout `fetch_src_pins.py` made at the pin: 4 anchors, 0 errors, 0 warnings, both licenses in the
root's `accepts:`; the GIC binding carries SPDX GPL-2.0, matching the `linux` entry. `spec_check.py`
with the docs and permissive roots as context roots and `--require-verified` accepts this record.
Licenses for the carried claims are as the earlier record found them. The addressing bullet is
bring-up-critical and needs the second verifier.

- Quick-facts/1 "Address translation in the tree": PASS — bcm2711.dtsi lines 10-11 give two
  address cells and one size cell at the root; the `soc` ranges (lines 41-43) map 0x7e000000 to
  0xfe000000 for 0x01800000 (24 MB), 0x7c000000 to 0xfc000000 for 0x02000000 (32 MB), 0x40000000
  to 0xff800000 for 0x00800000 (8 MB); dma-ranges (line 45) maps 0xc0000000 to 0x0 for 0x40000000.
  The inference's premises match the docs spec's Addressing model bullet (Low Peripheral ranges
  and full-map ranges as quoted), and all three targets fall in the Low Peripheral ranges, so the
  derivation holds; TODO present. Contrary glance: scb-bus (lines 550-551) maps 0x7c000000 with a
  larger 56 MB window and identity-maps 0x6_0000_0000; neither is claimed here and neither
  contradicts the conclusion.
- Quick-facts/2 "GIC node": PASS — lines 56-66: `arm,gic-400`, three interrupt cells, reg
  0x40041000/0x1000, 0x40042000/0x2000, 0x40044000, 0x40046000; translated through the ARM Local
  range to 0xff841000 and 0xff842000; maintenance interrupt GIC_PPI 9 with
  GIC_CPU_MASK_SIMPLE(4) and IRQ_TYPE_LEVEL_HIGH (PPI 9 is ID 25).
- Quick-facts/3 "PPI flags are not hardware polarity": PASS — now an `[inference]`. Premise 1
  holds: bcm2711.dtsi lines 64-65 encode the maintenance interrupt GIC_PPI 9 with
  IRQ_TYPE_LEVEL_HIGH and lines 446-453 encode the four timer PPIs with IRQ_TYPE_LEVEL_LOW.
  Premise 2 holds: the docs spec (at e7529fe) has a Quick-facts bullet "Interrupt IDs at the GIC"
  stating that PPI inputs are active-low level-sensitive, cited to the GIC-400 TRM §2.3.2, and the
  TRM's §2.3.2 does say all PPI signals are active-low level-sensitive; "fixed" is no longer
  claimed. Premise 3 holds: the nested anchor resolves at the pin (arm,gic.yaml lines 82-94, the
  third-cell flags under `#interrupt-cells`), and those lines say PPI configurability is
  implementation defined and that most SoCs of 2014 ignore the flag and use the hardware default.
  The derivation follows: inputs that share one polarity cannot all match two different flags, so
  the flags do not convey polarity, and the binding supports not expecting a write to take
  effect. `TODO (verify on hardware)` present. Contrary glance: the GIC-400 TRM's distributor
  register summary (§3.3, Table 3-2) lists GICD_ICFGR1 (the PPI word) as read-only with reset value 0x55540000,
  which agrees with the conclusion and means the hardware TODO should read back that value.
